Stated as information — software companies acquire data when the asset is hard to replicate, and acquire workflow apps to fold features into the platform.

Adobe → Semrush
2025-11 · Marketing analytics · $1,900M disclosed · ZI-rel 4 · value 4/5
data/signalun-buildable dataPlatform threatSW
Observed
Adobe acquired Semrush for $1.9B (disclosed, Nov 2025). Semrush is SEO/online-visibility and marketing competitive-intelligence data; implied multiple ~4.3x.
What it lets Adobe do in-house Interpreted
Lets Adobe bring marketing/competitive-intelligence data in-house for its digital-experience/marketing stack. Enables visibility/SEO and brand-performance signals natively alongside content and analytics.
ZoomInfo relationship Interpreted
Adjacent data-layer validation
A platform paying up for a proprietary marketing-data asset — supports the broad 'verified external data is strategically valuable' theme.
Limited direct overlap
Semrush is marketing/SEO data, not sales-prospecting/contact data; the ZoomInfo overlap is thematic, not product-level.
Counterargument Interpreted
Different data domain from ZoomInfo (marketing visibility vs B2B contact/intent). Read-through is to the value of data assets generally, not to ZoomInfo's specific market.
What would change the call: Adobe extending into B2B contact/account data, which would move this on-core.

HubSpot → Clearbit
2023-11 · B2B data enrichment · undisclosed · ZI-rel 5 · value 4/5
data/signalun-buildable dataClose compSW
Observed
HubSpot (CRM/marketing platform, ~$2.2B revenue) acquired Clearbit, a B2B data-enrichment provider that appends firmographic, technographic and contact data to records via API. Terms undisclosed. Rebranded inside HubSpot as “Breeze Intelligence.” HubSpot both competes with ZoomInfo in parts of the GTM stack and historically integrated third-party data vendors.
What it lets HubSpot do in-house Interpreted
Lets HubSpot enrich CRM records with company/contact data natively, rather than routing customers to a third-party data vendor (the role ZoomInfo plays for many teams). Enables enrichment as a built-in feature/credit and feeds HubSpot's own AI with first-party-controlled data. Means customers who bought a separate enrichment seat now have a native option in the tool they already use.
ZoomInfo relationship Interpreted
Enhances a competitor
Gives a CRM platform a ZoomInfo-style enrichment capability, narrowing one of ZoomInfo's differentiators (native integrated B2B data).
Reduces customers' need to buy ZoomInfo separately
For HubSpot-standardized accounts, good-enough native enrichment can substitute for a standalone seat — the seat-compression channel.
Counterargument Interpreted
HubSpot's base skews SMB/mid-market; ZoomInfo's depth targets enterprise. Clearbit's dataset is lighter than ZoomInfo's, so native-but-shallower enrichment may not displace ZoomInfo where coverage/quality is the buying criterion.
What would change the call: Evidence of ZoomInfo seat/logo losses at HubSpot-standardized accounts, or HubSpot materially closing the data-coverage gap.

Lets 6sense automate the SDR outreach workflow on top of its own intent data, rather than leaving prospecting to humans using separate tools. Enables an end-to-end loop — predict in-market accounts, then auto-engage — in one platform. Means the unit of work shifts from a human SDR toward an automated agent, changing what a seat is.
ZoomInfo relationship Interpreted
Compresses human seats
If agents do prospecting humans used to do, the number of reps each needing a data seat can fall — bearish for seat pricing.
Increases data consumption
An always-on agent needs continuously fresh contact/intent data — can raise programmatic/API pull, bullish for consumption pricing.
Counterargument Interpreted
Net direction depends on monetization model (seat vs consumption) and on whose data the agent uses. Saleswhale runs on 6sense's own data, so the consumption upside may accrue to 6sense, not ZoomInfo. Illustrates the tension more than resolves it.
What would change the call: ZoomInfo's mix shifting toward consumption/API revenue, or evidence of net seat declines at AI-SDR-adopting accounts.
